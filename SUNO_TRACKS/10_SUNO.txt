TITLE
10 — Судді тут не було

STYLE
Ukrainian-language theatrical rock opera, story-driven progressive hard rock, powerful clean rock vocals with clear Ukrainian diction, distorted electric guitar riffs, prominent electric bass, live acoustic drum kit, analog synthesizers, dramatic ensemble vocals, memorable melodic choruses, recurring musical motifs. No death growls, no classical-only arrangement. Rock-opera finale, starts at 92 BPM with broken heavy riff and urgent drums, then slow 68 BPM power-ballad epilogue in D minor. Female lead, clean ensemble chorus, expressive guitar solo, recurring opening melody transformed. Climactic revelation followed by a small quiet ending, no celebratory victory.

LYRICS
