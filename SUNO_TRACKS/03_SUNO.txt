TITLE
03 — Тисяча двісті

STYLE
Ukrainian-language theatrical rock opera, story-driven progressive hard rock, powerful clean rock vocals with clear Ukrainian diction, distorted electric guitar riffs, prominent electric bass, live acoustic drum kit, analog synthesizers, dramatic ensemble vocals, memorable melodic choruses, recurring musical motifs. No death growls, no classical-only arrangement. Ensemble arena rock anthem at 126 BPM, D minor with a bright F major chorus. Syncopated bass, driving live drums, huge layered clean gang vocals, melodic guitar lead. First exuberant collective victory; final bridge suddenly drops to ominous synth and half-time drums.
