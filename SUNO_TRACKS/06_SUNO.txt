TITLE
06 — Зламати Суддю

STYLE
Ukrainian-language theatrical rock opera, story-driven progressive hard rock, powerful clean rock vocals with clear Ukrainian diction, distorted electric guitar riffs, prominent electric bass, live acoustic drum kit, analog synthesizers, dramatic ensemble vocals, memorable melodic choruses, recurring musical motifs. No death growls, no classical-only arrangement. Central rock-opera villain-adjacent rally anthem, 132 BPM, D minor. Charismatic baritone, shouted clean gang responses, huge hook, galloping electric bass, heavy guitar riffs, live drums with theatrical stops. Seductive certainty becoming coercive. Not comedy, not death metal.
