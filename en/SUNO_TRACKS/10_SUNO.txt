TITLE
10 — There Was No Judge

STYLE
English-language theatrical rock opera, sung in English only, clear intelligible English diction, story-driven progressive hard rock, powerful clean rock vocals, distorted electric guitar riffs, prominent electric bass, live acoustic drums, analog synthesizers, dramatic ensemble vocals, memorable melodic choruses, recurring musical motifs. No death growls, no classical-only arrangement. Rock-opera finale in D minor. Begin at 92 BPM with a fractured heavy riff and urgent drums, then slow to a 68 BPM power-ballad epilogue. Female lead, clean ensemble, expressive electric guitar solo. Reprise the opening agents motif and the contact song. Revelation gives way to a quiet unresolved ending, not a victory anthem.
